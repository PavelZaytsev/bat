# Stage-0 direct-session results — 2026-08-15

These are exploratory, human-scored runs. The same frozen BDRv1 bundle and task packets were sent directly through non-streaming Chat Completions. No BAT controller, phase tools, or iteration budget was present.

| Candidate | Serving environment | Reasoning setting |

`xhigh` is an operational failure at the 8,192-token ceiling: it generated 36,231 reasoning characters and no final answer. Medium preserved the semantic quality and usually terminated, although the grouping probe still reached the token ceiling after producing a useful answer. This makes effort level and completion budget experimental variables; it does not justify adding a phase controller.

## Immediate interpretation

1. GPT-OSS-120B's earlier BAT failures were not evidence that it could not understand BDR. Direct BDRv1 runs were substantially better.
2. Qwen3.8-27B has enough semantic and meta-control capability to advance to a repository run.
3. Qwen's current weakness is verbosity and stopping discipline, not boundary formation.
4. The next experiment must include real file inspection, shell evidence, and tracker mutation. Prose success is only a gate to that test.
